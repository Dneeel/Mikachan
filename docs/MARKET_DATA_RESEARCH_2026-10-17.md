# Iranian market data review — 2026-10-17

## Decision and scope

`دلار`, `سکه`, and `ارز` are exact, authorized Utilities inputs in the existing aiogram bot. `/rate USD|EUR|AED|SEKE IRT` uses the same market feed. It reports indicative Toman quotes, the provider, and **fetch time**, while stating that the provider does not expose the underlying trade time. It does not call the downloader or create another update consumer. Official IRR reference pairs are refused with a clear explanation; the older `/rate USD EUR` and BTC/ETH global pairs remain separate.

The implemented no-key source is [Gheymat's documented public API](https://gheymat.online/price-api), queried once for its aggregated `GHEYMAT` IRT board and cached for 390 seconds. Its published free tier is 250 calls/day, so one running bot can refresh no more than 222 times/day under steady demand. Concurrent cache misses share one request. Quote symbols, Toman units, finite positive values, and upstream currency/category status are validated before a reply. The low and high of its two quote fields are shown as a range because the direction of `buy_price` and `sell_price` was not independently established. During a 2026-10-17 read-only probe, its USD rows differed substantially across TGJU, Navasan, AlanChand and Bonbast; the aggregate is an estimate, not an executable trading rate. The feed's `created_at` was recent even while a separate TGJU-derived dataset showed Thursday's cash-market observation, so `created_at` must not be advertised as trade time.

## The proposed spreadsheet formula

The supplied `IMPORTXML("https://alanchand.com/", "/html/body/main/section[5]/div[2]/div[1]/div/div/div[2]/span[1]/text()")` selected a **USDT/Tether card** when checked on 2026-10-17, not cash USD. That position depends on page layout and can change. Ten nested `SUBSTITUTE`s convert Persian digits only; Arabic digits, separators, locale-specific decimal marks, error pages, buy/sell direction, and timestamps remain unresolved. AlanChand has an [official JSON API](https://alanchand.com/media/api) with currency/gold/crypto categories and a bearer token. Use that when licensed access is available; do not treat an internal web AJAX endpoint as a public contract. Its [robots.txt](https://alanchand.com/robots.txt) disallows `/get-all-data`.

Recent [muchToman](https://github.com/doxigo/muchToman/blob/main/DEVELOPMENT.md) is a useful engineering reference: separate fiat and crypto source chains, named sources in every response, plausibility checks, and dropping unverified conversions. Its source mapping notes that Bonbast quotes JPY/AMD per 10 and IQD per 100 while TGJU uses per-unit values. [developerAmira's Telegram project](https://github.com/developerAmira/telegram-bot) likewise documents multi-source checks, unit normalization, provider health, and visibly stale fallback. These patterns are candidates for a later approved enhancement; their scraping and fallback sources must be checked for permission and precision before reuse. Small one-source bots and synthetic USDT-to-cash conversions are not evidence of accuracy.

## Best production path after provider access

1. Keep cash USD, bank transfer, USDT, physical coin, 18K gold per gram, and global crypto as **different asset types**. Store an explicit `price_unit`, `unit_size`, source, quote side, source observation time, fetch time, and market state for every quote. Convert IRR to Toman once using a documented unit; never use Google's official IRR midpoint as open-market cash USD.
2. Obtain a licensed direct Iranian market feed, starting with Navasan for its documented per-asset timestamps and explicit `usd_buy`/`usd_sell` or `sekkeh` codes. Run a real 7-day comparison trial against a second licensed feed such as AlanChand or Bonbast, spanning market hours, Friday/holiday closures, and provider outages. Compare observations at the same time; don't average unknown quote sides.
3. For crypto in Toman, take local exchange pairs directly (Wallex, then another licensed exchange) with verified quote units and current order book/ticker timestamps. Keep CoinGecko/global USD reference rates separate. Avoid fabricating a local crypto price by multiplying global USD by a stale cash dollar rate.
4. Add freshness gates using **source** observation time, not merely time of API fetch. If all valid sources fail, return an error or a clearly aged last-known quote. Drop implausible assets instead of zero-filling. Record source health without exposing keys. Cache with single-flight concurrency and provider quotas, as the current read path does.

The current public API integration is suitable for a small private bot's **indicative** quotes. It is not a verified high-frequency trading feed. A direct multi-provider system and more price assets require the owner's separate feature approval and, for licensed feeds, keys or a subscription.

## Non-downloader review

The single dispatcher still authorizes numeric user and group IDs before exact text, commands, or callbacks. Persian rate triggers are exact, so ordinary messages stay silent. Community cultural commands depend on an owner-reviewed, rights-cleared corpus; they do not fabricate jokes or poetry. Utilities process media on demand within bounded file/image sizes and two concurrent CPU-heavy slots; OCR language inspection and animated-sticker rendering now use those slots without blocking the event loop. Translation remains explicitly configured and external; a stronger privacy disclosure and provider health check can be considered before widening use. The next operational milestone remains terminal management, after acceptance of Community and Utilities.
